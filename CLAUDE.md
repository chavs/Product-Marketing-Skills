# Interview prep repo

This repo holds three skills for PMM interview prep: `pmm-senior-principal-interview-coach` (before an interview), `live-job-interview-assistant` (during one), and `pmm-case-study-assistant` (take-home case studies). Follow the rules below in every session here.

Generated research, guides, notes and drill logs go in `~/Documents/interview-prep/<company-slug>/`, outside the repo. Worktrees get recycled and untracked files in them are lost.
