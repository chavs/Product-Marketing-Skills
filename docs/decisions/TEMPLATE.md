# NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Accepted | Proposed | Deferred | Superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD (the day it was decided)
- Area: skills-architecture | live-assistant | case-study | coach | commands-and-tooling | working-rules | publishing | process
- Decided by: User | Agreed (assistant proposed, user approved) | Recorded from memory and commits (made in another session)
- Source: where it was decided (conversation date, memory note, commit)

## Context

What problem or trigger led to this. Two to four short sentences.

## Decision

What was decided, in plain words.

## Why

- The reasons and the evidence behind them.

## Alternatives rejected

- The option, and why it was rejected. Write "None recorded." if none were discussed.

## Consequences and follow-ups

- What this changes. Anything still open or unverified.

## Implemented in

- File paths, commits and pull requests.
